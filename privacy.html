2. Categories of Data Collected

The website rnlmkt.com collects the following categories of personal data:

  • Data voluntarily submitted via the contact form: name, email address, and any message entered by the user in the embedded Google Form in the “Contact” section of the site.
  • Technical data automatically collected by the hosting infrastructure (Vercel): IP address, browser and device type, requested pages, access date and time, system logs needed for the operation and security of the site.
  • Browsing and statistical analytics data (Google Analytics 4): device/cookie identifier, pages visited, visit duration, traffic source. Collected only after prior consent — see Section 8.
  • WhatsApp conversation data: phone number and content of the messages sent by the user who voluntarily chooses to contact the Controller via the WhatsApp link on the site.

4. Processing Methods and Security Measures

Data is processed using electronic and IT tools, with logic strictly related to the purposes indicated above and in such a way as to ensure its security and confidentiality.

rnlmkt.com is a static site with no database of its own for the server-side storage of user data: the data entered in the contact form is stored directly on Google Forms; conversations started via WhatsApp are stored on WhatsApp/Meta’s servers. The Controller adopts the following measures: connection always secured via the HTTPS/TLS protocol; hosting on the Vercel infrastructure with industry-standard security measures; access to the data limited to the Controller alone.

5. Recipients and Transfer of Data to Non-EU Countries

To provide the site and its related services, the Controller uses the following third parties, which may be based or process data outside the European Economic Area (EEA):

  • Vercel Inc. (United States) — provider of the site’s hosting infrastructure. Vercel Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (DPF), the mechanism recognised by the European Commission as an adequate safeguard for the transfer of data to the United States.
  • Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC (United States) — provider of the contact form (Google Forms) and the statistical analytics service (Google Analytics 4). Google adheres to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or applies the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission as the legal basis for the transfer of data.
  • WhatsApp Ireland Limited / Meta Platforms, Inc. (United States) — messaging service used at the user’s own initiative. The processing of data exchanged via WhatsApp is governed by the WhatsApp/Meta privacy policy, which provides its own safeguards for the international transfer of data.

The Controller does not sell or transfer users’ personal data to third parties for those third parties’ own commercial purposes.

6. Retention Period

Data collected via the contact form is retained for a maximum period of 1 (one) year from the date of the last interaction with the user, unless a longer period is required to fulfil legal obligations (e.g. retention of accounting/tax documentation, according to the terms provided by the applicable legislation).

Analytics data collected via Google Analytics 4 is retained for a maximum period of 14 (fourteen) months.

7. Data Subject Rights

As a data subject, the user has the right, under the conditions provided by Articles 15-22 GDPR, to:

  • access their personal data (right of access, Art. 15);
  • obtain the rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16);
  • obtain the erasure of data, in the cases provided for (right to be forgotten, Art. 17);
  • obtain the restriction of processing (Art. 18);
  • obtain the portability of the data (Art. 20);
  • object to the processing (Art. 21), in particular for consent-based statistical analysis purposes, which may be withdrawn at any time without affecting the lawfulness of the processing carried out before the withdrawal.

To exercise these rights, the user may contact the Controller at the address indicated in Section 1.

The data subject also has the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it), should they consider that the processing of their personal data has taken place in breach of the applicable legislation.
