I am an AI-fluent international marketing consultant for ambitious start-ups, e-commerce brands, and growing businesses that want to move faster, think sharper, and build brands with real momentum. I combine brand strategy, content marketing, social media, and digital campaigns with a practical command of AI strategy and tools.

I help businesses use AI where it creates genuine value: research, audience insight, positioning, content production, campaign planning, analysis, and decision-making. The goal is not AI for its own sake, but smarter marketing systems that improve speed, quality, and consistency. By implementing strategic direction and structured execution, I bring the capability of a modern marketing team into a smart and focused partnership, across English, Dutch, and Italian.

Over the past decade, I have developed brands, communities, and campaigns across the cycling, outdoor, and lifestyle sectors. My approach starts with people: what they value, what earns their attention, and what motivates them to act. Based in Varese, Italy, I work with businesses across Europe.

The Work

A selection of projects from recent years. From conversion-focused websites to global community and ambassador programs — I turn positioning and content into growth you can measure.

Web · Personal brand

Turning a freelance web developer's site into a booked-call engine

Reworked positioning, messaging and page structure for Fabio Angelici's freelance web-developer site — sharper hero copy, clearer service framing, and a conversion-first layout with prominent CTAs. Paired with on-page SEO and analytics tracking to turn passive visitors into booked intro calls.

↑ 16% CTR on the primary CTA · 23% lift in conversion rate (CRO) · 30% more organic traffic in first 2 months

Community

Scaling a global cycling community for ASSOS

Developed and ran a global community engagement strategy for ASSOS — pairing always-on social content with in-person rides through the ASSOS Speed Club (now Collective Thirteen). Grew and activated the community across Strava, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and TikTok, partnering with distributors, ambassadors, and influencers to deepen loyalty and open new growth markets.

↑ 75% engagement · Strava club 10.8k → 50k (+363%) · Instagram 103k → 136k followers · in <16 months

Ambassador program

Launching Liv Cycling's Italian ambassador program for Giant Group

Developed and ran the Italian ambassador program for Liv Cycling, Giant Group's dedicated women's cycling brand — recruiting riders across road, gravel, MTB, enduro, downhill and triathlon to shape community strategy, create educational and product content, and organize training camps and group rides.

↑ 5–10 ambassadors across 6 disciplines · Instagram grew ~100 → 15k followers · 2 MTB training camps (~25 riders each) · numerous ambassador-led group rides
